Trading with real money

This page trades real money directly from your browser on Hyperliquid, by default. It connects your own wallet (or an in-app wallet you create here), never holds your funds, and only ever trades - it cannot withdraw. The steps are in the sidebar's Hyperliquid panel:

  1. Set Source to Hyperliquid (already selected by default) and start on Testnet to practise with fake money first.
  2. Under Connect using, connect a browser wallet (MetaMask etc.) and sign one approval message, or create an in-app wallet - either way the trading key can place and close orders but cannot withdraw, and never leaves this browser tab.
  3. Fund the wallet (see the Fund tab for a live readiness check), press Test connection, tick the acknowledgement, and press Enable real trading.
  4. Keep this tab open and online - stop-losses only run while the page stays open. A web page cannot trade while closed.

Want it running on an always-on machine instead, or trading on a different exchange via API key? A separate Survivor Bot server app is included in the download bundle (SurvivorBot.zip: run pip install -r requirements.txt then python web.py) with the same strategy, goal, optimiser and AI assistant, plus SELL ALL & HALT, and a Dockerfile for 24/7 hosting.

Start on Testnet, then a small amount you can afford to lose entirely. No strategy guarantees profit, and you can lose all of your capital.

Connect other chains

Arbitrum, Ethereum, BNB Chain, Monad, Tron and Bitcoin here are for viewing an address and its balance only - this app does not place trades on them (only Hyperliquid and Binance, in the sidebar, run the automated strategy - Hyperliquid itself runs on Arbitrum, which is why it's connected the same way, with MetaMask). Solana is the exception: once Phantom is connected you can run a manual swap below, quoted and routed through Jupiter's public aggregator and signed by Phantom itself - it is not part of the automated bot, each swap is one-off and needs your confirmation in Phantom every time.

Bitcoin, Tron and Solana use completely different signing schemes from Ethereum's - a MetaMask key cannot control funds on those chains, and vice versa. Only send each asset to an address on its own native network.

How it works

Scans the most liquid USDT markets each cycle.

Buys when price closes below the lower Bollinger Band with RSI under 30 and a bounce candle, skipping coins that fell over 20% in a day.

Sells at take-profit, at the moving average, on RSI over 70, at the stop, or after 48 hours. Stop moves to breakeven after +3%.

Survives by sizing each trade so a stop-out costs about the risk % of equity, capping any coin at 25%, pausing 6 h after a stop, halving size after 3 losses in a row, stopping new trades after a 5% down day, and liquidating and halting if equity falls to 70% of the start.

Fees (0.26%) and slippage (0.05%) are simulated.
